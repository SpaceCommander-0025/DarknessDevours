Summary

Ronjaree aka RJ, Edwin aka Eddie, and Allayane have all found themselves wondering into the same place after a journey across Nocctherra for many different reasons. They all get to the muddy mess of the shabby Town of Ashmaw. A place where there isn't much to see except for the desolate dark mud that now cakes their boots and pants. The Hideaway Tavern is the only warm glimmering part of their introduction to the sorry excuse for a town. And so they all find themselves inside the warm Tavern looking for food and drink and perhaps some lodging. A sweet lady bartender finds them all at the same table and asks, "What'll it be?" The orders are fast. RJ wants porridge and ale. Eddie ale and porridge. And Allayane, well, he wants mead and warm bread, plus a little more if you please. The bartender gives him a wink and when she walks back gives Allayane a bit of a show.

While they are waiting, complete strangers a woman bursts through the Tavern doors. Her eyes aglaze with a fire in looking for something. She scans until her eyes set upon the three newcomers. She just got words some adventurers had made their way into town. So she beelines it too their table and sits staring them all down. "Are you all looking for work? Your adventurers right?" They learn the woman's name is Cressida Ursolo, a neighborring terraforming and mining rig Captain in her own rite. She relays the distress call from her husband.

Captain's Distress Log

She relays offering 3,000GP for the investigation into the disappearance of her husband and his crew. The adventurers agree and go with Cressida to get more information and suit up. Cressida provides the team with mining dive suits that deal with the pressure and environment down below. She also provides each adventurer with a crew patch that will allow them access to the station and the ability to talk with Cadence, the rig computer. Ronjaree tries to convince Cressida to offer them more money for this task, however Cressida replies that the 3,000 GP is their life savings and all she has to offer. She adds that if they succeed, whenever they come through Ashmaw, they will have room and board and all expenses covered for their stay plus some goodies from the Crevasse. This seems to satisfy RJ and the rest of the team. They dawn their suits and Allayane asks if the suits come in a more fashionable color. Cressida confused let's them know that the station is at 600 ft. They will go down in a roundabout area of where the station is located.
Upon walking them to the edge of the Crevasse, there is a rickety old elevator that will transport them down to the dive elevator. Cressida bids them adue and they begin the descent into the Crevasse. Eddie leans over the side making the entire platform tilt. The suits are heavy and thick which makes walking an exercise. Once at the edge of the water Eddie discovers the red and orange flakes that suggests some volcanic activity down below along with other minerals in the water from the mines. They then attach to the dive elevator with weights on at full speed ahead. Eddie figures out how to slow their descent and how to read the beacon that they have to navigate. They get stopped at 600 ft and begin to catch their bearings. Navigating down here is a crap shoot even with Allayane's Darkvision. The begin to move eastly and a signal begins to chime. They investigate further and are able to make out Alpha station from the red emergency lights. The HQ is completely dark on the inside as they make their way to the airlock. Once inside and as the water retracts, they get out of the big dive suits to explore the station. In the HQ, they discover Cami Ruther, the Cheif Engineer that Cressida mentioned getting in touch with. Cami is unconscious in the oxygen filtration room with an oval shaped rigging aparatus. Allayane is able to stabilize Cami but unable to wake her. Eddie finds the original dive log in some papers strewn across the floor.
They then begin to investigate the rest of the station including Dive Command where all Dive Suits have been launched and there is only 1 functioning Escape Pod. One dive suit remains in good condition in the dive command along with 34 oxygen tanks. Cadence is sure to tell the team that the oxygen is declining consistently as there is no power to the station. He also relays Cami's Crew Profile and her recorded Introduction video for the adventurer's.

Cami Profile

Eddie takes a look farther into the station discovering a connection to Bravo Station. The hallway connector is damaged and water is trickling in, but the disturbing part is a red, vein like mesh that has begin to make its way into the station. RJ and Allayane check out Medical Bay finding many of Sophie's Notes, Sophie's Recordings, and two of the crew that have been afflicted by this mysterious creature. Norman and Marion are in stasis chambers and it seems that the doctor on board was trying to cure them of the infection and was getting closer, but unsuccessful to that point.

Sophie's Findings

Eddie takes a look at the mess hall and notices that whatever happened here, happened fast. Then moves on to look at the Research Lab. There he finds two large eggs, one that is complete and one that has been dissected. There is also a device in the room that is the Resonance Engine built by Sophie Bouchard, the doctor, and Cami Ruther, the Chief Engineer. After finding some more notes and the operations guide, the adventurers begin trying to figure out how the machine works. They tend to Cami and get her to medical and provide her with a Floatation Serum. Then they move both Marion and Norman into the Research Lab and begin setting up the calibrations for utilizing the Resonance Engine. After a little bit of thought and scouring Sophie's notes, they are able to get the machine to light up green and it looks ready to perform the procedure.

Summary

RJ, Eddie, and Allayane all prepare in the Research Bay as they have gotten the Resonance Engine working and seems to be calibrated. RJ discovers that there are 6 containment containers in the corner. They proceed to attach the containers to the stasis pods that house Marion and Norman. They then turn on the machine and the engine rumbles and erupts in black smoke as it begins working. The red veins on both Marion and Norman's bodies begin to retract from the extremities and coalese in clump that is moving towards the port on the stasis tubes. The veins emerge and splattered into the containment containers, however both containers glass begins to crack from the force of the exit. The veins crumple onto the floor.

A fight ensues between the party and the Veins. The all work together to get the veins contained by the means of many Mage Hands and of course Ronjaree's infamous tridents packing a punch. They are able to contain the first Vein without too much trouble although Eddie gets a personalized smooch from the creature as it tries latching onto anything it can grab onto. Allayane notices a gun like device hanging on the side of the engine. He picks it up and realizes that it fires. So he points it at the other Vein and fires. Unfortunately, the Vein opens and the pulse fires right through. It's attention lands on Allayane and his hands goes up waving with a sheepish smile, "Hi!"

After another few moments of failed and tried again recapture of the Vein, they finally succeed in capturing both of the Veins. Eddie places both of the Veins inside the dissected egg so that they have a much harder time breaking the glass. With the extraction complete, Norman and Marion's vitals begin to stabilize as Norman begins to regain consciousness. The team takes them both back to the pod chamber and the excess gelatin is cleansed from the inside of the pods. Norman and Marion are cleaned up and now rest in the Medical Bay. RJ and Allayane ask Norman a few questions and he is very skeptical of who they are. It comes across as more ungrateful. He reveals that the last thing he remembers is other crew caught in the vines and the Captain was battling something in the water. Allayane notices that Cami is stirring and goes over to wake the woman up. He grabs her shoulders and shakes her awake. Slap, a bright red mark adorns his face as Cami comes too. She asks who they are and Allayane retorts holding his cheek, "We are your rescue team, bitch!" After the tensions die down, Allayane, RJ and Cami get one of the Dragon Eggs to the oxygen filtration system and return power to the station. Eddie stays to attend to Norman and Marion. Norman asks for water and Eddie goes galavanting around the Mess Hall. He grabs a couple jackets and a couple glasses of water along with finding Norman's wedding rings. Eddie pockets those for the time being and gets back to the others.

The team speaks with Cami as she works with Cadence to get the systems partially back online. She remains a resource over the comms should they need her and will prepare for the final evacuation. Cami also let's them know a little about the Dragon Turtles. They have never attacked any of the crew. The team decides to continue exploring and get to the rest of the crew.

Back out in the water, they begin following the signals and discover some unique places. Cliff Site I and II right along with Charlie Station. They discover an emergency beacon.

Reese's Distress Log

They also rescue Reese Daraway from being trapped in the diving elevator line beneath Cliff Site II. They hustle back to Cliff Site I to utilize the emergency site to take care of Reese. They take Reese out of her dive suit as some of the vein have attached to the outside of it. Allayane does some mouth to mouth to get her breathing again. This works, but Allayane is pushed off when Reese wakes up. "Who are you all?" She is just as skeptical of them as Norman and Cami upon waking. She comes to thank them for helping her and urges them to find the rest of the crew. And she asks Allayane not to kiss her again as she is taken. This doesn't seem to matter to Allayane and Reese wataches him a bit more carefully from then on. Reese stays at the emergency site to keep in contact with them and provide further assistance if needed. The team decide to take an extra dive suit with them in case it is needed.

The team continues exploring and comes upon Charlie Station. They enter the station and encounter Sophie Bouchard and a critical Missy French. Sophie is overjoyed that the rescue team is here and has rescued Reese and those in Alpha Station. She is also surprised to here that Norman and Marion are no longer afflicted by the Veins. She explains that they were attacked by something in the water. She lost Reese and Taylor is the struggle. The team gives Sophie the extra dive suit to be able to transport Missy back to Alpha Station for emergency surgery. Meanwhile, the team goes to investigate some of the crew quarters finding personnel effects of each of the crew.

Summary

RJ, Eddie, and Allayane all adventure out into the water, but are being pulled along by the current. They scramble to attach to the Crevasse wall. RJ is able to deploy the pocket anchor which latches her to the wall as Allayne finds a hold of the wall. Eddie has struggles trying to hold onto the wall. They discover gemstones in the walls and they spend a minute mining a few of the gemstones making each of them collect a small bag of about 30 a piece. They try to catch themselves as they realize a giant whirlpool in the middle of the crevasse. The walls widen down here so there isn't much to keep them from flowing in. Allyane is able to catch the final part of the wall to keep them from disappearing into the current, however the strain on the rope intensifies.

Eddie's rope breaks and he disappears into the whirlpool. At that Allayane and RJ let go to go into the whirlpool together. No one arrives in the Dragon Turtle Cavern injured, but they finally realize that they are in the home of the Ancient Dragon Turtles. Allayane knows the most about them and that Paleontologists in Nocctherra only have skeletons to study. RJ and Eddie investigate a ship wreck and are able to loot a couple books of magic, one RJ is able to decipher is Speak to the Dead. The other is not decipherable in the moment. They also find a pouch with 10 Diamonds, 10 Rubies, and 30 Amethyst gems in it. A common spellcasting item bundle for Necromancer's. The also find the Abyssal Compass that points in the direction of the most magical significance.

They proceed to try and communicate with the Adult Dragon Turtle who appears to be protecting Taylor Cohlid. She has a large gash through her suit that seems to be clotted and no longer bleeding. RJ and Allayane try to sing and talk there way over to Taylor. Upon Allayane displaying some healing magic, this is enough to lower the female Dragon Turtles boundaries and lifts its flipper to reveal Taylor below. And when Allayane's spell fails again the female Dragon Turtle breathes into his face and his spell instantly succeeds. Taylor wakes up and gets the opportunity to talk with the group. She explains that these Caverns run all through the Crevasse. There are other ways down here that isn't the whirlpool and there are smaller vents that can get them up and down. Allayane patches up Taylor's suit as she insists that she still has to go down to help her friends and crewmates. She is relieved when the party tells her that Reese is fine. She also reveals that the crew have been into these Caverns a number of times and interact with the Dragon Turtles and have formed a unique bond with them. That is why they are protective of the crew.

RJ asks how they go down further and Taylor points to another shallow pool. They all good together and wind up coming out at the Geothermal Vents. An extrememly heated area of water that does have some magmatic eruptions. They find Leslie, Vincent, Claude, and Alvin, cocooned inside these large vein trees. The cocoons are rather easy to break open and they are able to get Leslie, Claude, and Vincent out successfully before a Aboleth shows up to stop them. It comes from the tears in world leading to Shadovar. The party has heard some of the general rumors about Shadovar. It is a mirror realm and that which inhabits it is up to no good. The cost of entering Shadovar is a soul. A battle ensues with the Aboleth as Taylor is able to get Leslie to the pool that will float them back to the station. Eddie and Allayane manages to get Vincent and Claude to this pool as well. Alvin is still cocooned inside the vein tree and the Aboleth has charmed Allayane to help it. RJ has taken the Aboleth on head first and it trying to give the others a chance to get Alvin and escape.

Taylor is able to cut the vines off his throat and starts to drag him towards the whirlpool. RJ begins doing long sweeps with her battle axe to clear away the Veins from the Dragon Turtles shell trying to free it.

Eddie and Allayane take on the Aboleth getting in some great sneaky shots and manage to reduce its size to make it more managable. Allayane decides to sneak up and get a close up shot, but soon realizes that being the hero is a bad idea as the Aboleth turns on him. It grabs Allayane and manages to latch on squeezing with enough force that Allayane goes limp in its tentacles. Some of his memories are extracted. Eddie tries to assist but ends up missing. Taylor is able to dump the Captain into whirlpool and goes to help Allayane. She takes her harpoon and fires twice with the attached lance. She fires and the lance strikes into the Aboleth's side grappling it. Taylor pulls it away from Allayane and towards herself. This gives Eddie and RJ the opportunity to finish it off as Allayane barely grips onto what is left of his life force.

With the Dragon Turtle free from the Veins and all the crew extracted from the cocoons, the surrounding tree like structures lose all their color growing a pale white. All the Veins attached to the tears follow suit. Everything grows white and they succeeded in closing the tears. The crew manages to get themselves to the whirlpool and Taylor waits to make sure that no one gets left behind. They all pass through the whirlpool and come out very close to Alpha and Bravo Stations. They get everyone back to Alpha station where they are greeted by Cami and Sophie. Sophie gets to work taking a look at the Captain and the Infected crew. Taylor gets the breath knocked out of her when Reese plows into Taylor's arms. They share a sweet kiss and a long hug. Then Reese makes her way over to Allayane and Eddie giving them each a kiss on the cheek. And gives RJ a hard hug as a sincere thank you for all that they have done for her and this crew. Sophie asks for their help with removing the infections as she has been able to weaponize the energy flow and determined that the Veins had a central heart that when struck can kill them. Eddie, RJ, and Allayane all help and are able to successfully remove the Infections from Vincent, Leslie, and Claude. Unfortunately, Alvin didn't make it through the procedure as the Vein sensed it was trying to be removed and killed the host. Allayane decides to share Alvin's love note with Marion. She takes a moment to read it, a few tears fall over her cheek. Marion thanks Allayane for this and wishes he would have said something sooner.

The news hit the crew hard at first, but they are were openly grateful for the help of Eddie, RJ, and Allayane. Indebted to this small band of adventurers who risked everything to help save them. Cami prepares the life pod which can only carry 12 people. RJ, Eddie, Taylor, and Reese all volunteer to swim back to the surface using the diving elevator in order to make enough room for everyone including Alvin. The get back to the surface and find medics and people ready and waiting to help. Cressida bounds into her husbands arms as the rest of the crew seem to do the same. She readily thanks the party for helping get her husband and the crew back alive. She is saddened to hear of Alvin's passing, but knows the odds of everyone nearly coming back is astounding that the party only lost one. Cressida pays them the 3,000GP as was offered at the beginning and anytime they are in the area whatever they need, she and the crew will take care of. RJ, Eddie, and Allayane all return some of the items to the crew. RJ makes sure that Sohpie got one of her romance novels, Into the Deep, back. Sophie looks quite embarrassed asking RJ if she read it. RJ says no and Sophie looks relieved. A small consolation. Eddie returns the Captain's photo album to Cressida and Marcus, but keeps the cool dive watch. Allayane returns Vincent's journal and Leslie's homemade newspaper. Eddie returns some of Reese's Drawings including the colored Dragon and a few of Taylor. Taylor rubs her eyes as she didn't need to see herself in Reese's drawings anymore than she already does. Reese is over the moon with the kind gesture. RJ keeps the Pocket Anchor as Eddie also keeps Buddy the Turtle and Norman's Rings. Vincent makes a note that they are having a little ceremony for Alvin in the next couple of days to honor him and the live that he lived and invites the party. Eddie gathers himself and turns into The Amazing Marvelo and tries speaking to the dead to see if Alvin will come forth. It takes a moment before Eddie feels a cool hand squeeze his shoulder. "I'll take this from here." Alvin does come and Marion is the only one that truly believes in what Eddie is doing. So she watches. Eddie tells the crew that Alvin is here. They all stand silently and look toward Marion. Alvin kisses her cheek and she shudders in surprise as she lifts her hand over her cheek. Her eyes wide with wonder. Alvin turns to Eddie, "Tell her she is loved and I will always be around." Eddie nods and Alvin takes another good look at his crew and at Marion before he disappears at peace with everything.

Afterwards, the party is able to part ways from the crew noticing that the local people of Ashmaw no longer treat them as outsiders. They had become a part of the life and culture of Ashmaw and are shown the way to Walk the Past. A tavern and room and board for the party. Cressida helps Eddie get a crossbow. The party take some time to recover and are free to chase their next adventure. Together.
