Summary

RJ, Eddie, and Allayane start a brand new day in Ashmaw. Eddie is no where to be found as he skips off to Oldie's Magic, the only magic shop in town. While there Eddie meets Fredrick 'Oldie' Oldan who is the owner of the shop. Eddie inquires about how to learn the spell Speak to the Dead from the spell book. Oldie let's Eddie know that learn these kinds of spells usually require helping a soul in some way so that they can move on or solve unfinished business. So helping a soul and they may choose to help you learn the spell. Oldie tries to sell Eddie on some frivalous junk from the Crevasse until something changes in Oldie and he presents a chest full of shiny pearls. Eddie picks out a beautiful, large Silver pearl that he can see himself in. But Oldie has some strong magic in him and this makes Eddie nervous since he may have taken something of Oldie's once a upon a time. So Eddie quickly takes his leave of that shop.

RJ and Allayane wake up to find that Eddie is gone. Allayane quickly dies his hair blonde and jets out with RJ to get some breakfast at Crevasse Delights. They meet Arma Delight, dark black braider hair with silver strands wrapped throughout and bright blue eyes, who takes their order and gets them something to eat. A Gooseberry Danish and a Whale Bread Stew. Arma's daughter Naomi rushes through the door with seemingly exciting news about her adventures with the Guardians. Her mother brushes Naomi off and focuses on tending to the needs of her customers. Naomi keeps an eye on the party. Eddie meets up with them and manages to order a Whalte Bread Stew, Crab Pot, and an Eel Roll. When they leave, Allayane winks at Naomi as she is watching from around the corner. She darts back behind the wall at Allayane's gesture.

They party visits a few of the other shops. They go to Bits and Pots where they meet an eccentric man, Gaten Austenwild. He seemingly isn't all there, but a kind soul at his core. He ends up selling one heavy black stone and a fur cloak to Allayane and gives Eddie a sheer Dark Blue sparkling cloak that is reminescent of the night sky for free. He is easily distracted and ends up playing with the heavy black rocks when they decide to leave.

They venture to the Hospital and there they met Caylen. She helps them purchase some healing potions for their adventures. They also get to say hi to Dr. Sophie Bouchard again and inquire about the time of the funeral. She is happy to see them and tells them around 3p. She wishes them the best and looks forward to seeing them around.

They then venture to Sader's General and meet the owner Sader Ben and his son, Sawder. They are intrigued by a beautiful revolver that has dark blue framework with accenting ocean blue components. It is engraved with the names of the locals. Sader's starting price is 1,500 GP but after some hagggling, he drops it to 1,250 GP. The party puts a pin in it for now. The gets 10 days worth of rations for three people of dried meats, vegetables, and fruits. Sawder comes out to package up the items and RJ gets two more Javelins as well. The entire party notices a tattoo on his forearm, The Shadow Guardians. RJ and Allayane start asking him questions and he sets outside. The party learn that there is a group called the Shadow Guardians, but aren't able to get anything else out of Sawder except the fact that he doesn't trust them. He doesn't speak the kindest to RJ and Allayane tries to do some child rearing. Sawder takes off and the party can't catch him as he bounds off and across the rooftops. 'Still don't trust you.' He retorts as he disappears.

From there, they attend Alvin's funeral. Gaten places a pot full of trinkets and parts that Alvin always bought from him on his grave. Many of the townsfolk come out right along with the crew. Alvin's grave is a small rendition of Alvin in statue form that is big enough to hold his mining helmet on top. The helmet itself says ‘Gone Ahead. Probably up to something’. A painted blue and purple swirl is decorated on the other side. But each part of the statue is made from discarded materials from the junkyard as Alvin always loved finding and making something new from the discarded. There are little jokes that Alvin told the crew that are tied to the statue. Captain Ursolo says a few words placing a pocket knife that Alvin had once sold him onto the statue. Marion also reads the love letter that he wrote her and tucks it into the statue. A few of the townspeople talk about memories with Alvin.

Meanwhile, Eddie has communed with the spirits when he entered the graveyard. This is a full place, full of the dead. He sees white, dark, and blue translucent beings. The necromnacers have white or dark look to them as the more common ghost are the blue translucent. Eddie spots Alvin and eventually follows Alvin over where he is talking to another ghost, Abigail. Eddie says hi and she greets him. She is surprised that he can actually see them. She definitely has a repore with Alvin and initially confused why he was there. She ends up leaving abruptly and Eddie inquires with Alvin about learning Speak to the Dead. He says that he is fine, but perhaps Eddie could help Abigail. If Eddie can find her again that is. She likes to hangout at Carl's Hideout. Then he splits and leaves.

After the funeral, they check out the job boards and the campsites. Eddie learns that the adventurers aren't fond of a group called the Shadow Guardians as most of the local looting spots are already looted because of them. Carl's Hideout seems to be a hotspot although no one can seemingly get inside. There are rumors that something lies beneath the Crypt of Fame in the Cemetery and possible magic items in the Junkyard. But nothing is for sure. The adventurer's are a routy bunch and can see why the locals aren't their biggest fans. They get dinner at Crevasse Delights with a bowl of stew and a pint in Alvin's honor. The meals are free that evening to honor Alvin. Unfortunately, Naomi doesn't make an appearance.

They go to check out the Crypt of Fame as darkness is beginning to settle in. The find names upon the right side of the wall that are those remembered and lost. The left side of the wall is the families wall. Names are inscribed under the Deepwoods, Austenwild, Ben, and Geon. RJ finds a scroll and picks it up. Immediately, RJ is transported to a dark black void and is nearly frozen when she returns and hits the ground. Powerful dark magic resides in the scroll and RJ sees a vision of her father that gives her a warning. 'Nevermind what happened. Focus on what's ahead. Stay away from the spirits.' The party finds SG remembers inscribed into the stone wall.

They return to Walk the Past Tavern with the scroll and vowing that visiting the Crypt at night is probably not a good idea. The find the Geon sisters, Millicent and Curra, there ready to lock up for the evening. The party catches sight of Millie's tattoo on the back of her neck, a grim reaper with an SG inscribed inside. Curra has one just behind her ear. The team starts to ask question and Millie explains that this is something she cannot share. That they need to stop asking questions about the SG. Millie makes RJ cleanse her hands as she has been marked by a dark powerful magic. Millie believes this magic belongs to an Ancient and the only way to destroy it is to destroy the Ancient themselves. Millie offers a blood rite to help protect RJ, but in return the party would need to stopping seeking out the SG. The party declines, but lets Millie and Curra keep the scroll for the time being. They head off to bed as Millie and Curra have quite the story to tell their compatriots. Millie reveals that her and her sister are Dark Necromancer's and that they are the last of their line in its entirity. They do not trust anyone outside their circle. Millie offeres nothing but respect to the party, however she doesn't budge when it comes to protecting those she cares about.

Key Events

  • Explored the Town of Ashmaw
  • Meet many of the locals around the town.
  • Crevasse Delights food grants temporary hit points to those who dine there.
  • Have discovered a secret group of young adults that seem to be apart of a group called the Shadow Guardians.
  • Discovered a scroll imbued with Ancient Dark Magic.
