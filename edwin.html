Backstory

Eddie grew up feeling insignificant. This feeling was further enforced when his parents pushed him out of the home in Coldrest when he came of age so they wouldn't have to support him anymore. He joined a troupe of traveling performers in Cinder who taught him how to perform, how to use a makeup & disguise kit, how to forge papers, & how to steal from pockets & nearby stores during others' performances. (He is especially drawn to shiny things.) During his time with them, he created the persona of The Amazing Marvelo, Master of Mysteries (a fake medium). Over the years, he perfected his performance (& thief skills) even after the troupe eventually dispersed (due to suspicion from authorities).

As Eddie, he is short & unremarkable, blending in to the crowd. At night, he dons makeup, costume & platform shoes to become Marvelo. Occasionally, he uses his makeup/ disguise kit to disguise himself as others. Once, he even forged his own pardon while disguising himself as the local mayor! He laughed all the way out of town. Then during one performance, he commanded the dead to speak to him & heard an ethereal voice answer. That wasn't him. Wait! This is real?! Fascinating! His performances now take on a new light. Sometimes the spirits cooperate, most times they don't.

One day, he hears rumors of strange occurences tied to the Ashmaw Crevasse, & he's drawn to the mystery. He must investigate and he meets the party while sitting close to their table.

Eddie is mesmerized by shiny things, & he's ALWAYS temped to steal them even if they aren't valuable. He grinds his teeth when he knows it isn't in his best interest to do so, & uses Mage Hand to procure them when he loses the inner battle.

Eddie is insecure that he's enough. When he feels unsure of himself, he overcompensates, tells jokes, or jumps into his persona of The Amazing Marvelo. His character arc will be learning that he's more loved & respected as Eddie, not Marvelo.
