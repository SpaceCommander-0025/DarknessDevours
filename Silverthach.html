Life in Silverthach

Silverthach is a small, but bustling little town. The open markets are an oasis from the hot, dry desert that surrounds them. Silver. Silver. Silver. Their only marketable property for which the world of Nocctherra competes to get the best quality and quantity of silver products.
The weather is often fair, yet the mornings and evenings are the best times for enjoying Silverthach as everything shuts down for the heat of the day. Many of the Sand Divers can capture water from the silver mines deep below and with a reverse of pressure and a bit of magic produce the towns water supply. Right along with Silver, water is the most expensive commodity especially if you are an outsider.
Every building in Silverthach has a silver coated thatch roof. Often a wandering drifter through the dunes will tell the tale of how they saw the City of Mirrors or the City of Light shining into their eyes and their salvation from the desert was granted.

Jaquette's Song

Allayne Jaquette, the town hero. His sounds have faded, but his legend still thrives amongst the people. His poems and songs are sung from the open music market down to the dripping silver mines. He who stood against the brigands with only song and instrument as his weapon. The town saved. And now, many years later, they still chant his name when he comes to town and he never pays a thing when he resides there.

Argenta

The Argenta are a faction of master craftsmen and blacksmiths that create items from pure silver for profit all around Nocctherra. They take the Rite of the Sand and are granted the ability to dive through the sand and stone of the earth by the god, Tharos. Those that are not considered worthy have specialized suits that can displace the sand long enough to transport a person wearing the suit deep into the sand and eventually reach the mine. Many a souls have been lost deep beneath the sand, but are honored through blood sacrifice by the Argenta.
The Rite of the Sand is a ritual that results in a silver branded tattoo. The process happens in three stages. The Cutting, the removal of the first layer of the design most commonly the skin. The Branding. The molten silver is poured into the groove of where the skin has been removed. The Rite. Touched by the blood of the Argenta and sanctioned in the eyes of Tharos. Once the ritual has been performed, the new person has now become a new Argentum.

The Stone Oath

Amongst the Argenta are an extreme sect known as The Stone Oath. They are Warrior-monks who bind themselves to vows of service. Their extremism varies—some noble, some zealots.

The Broken Spine

Jagged stone ridges jutting from the earth, forming natural walls and battlements.Oaths carved here cannot be broken.
